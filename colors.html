1 · Luminance

Grayscale Voronoi caustics, multi-scale. Edges glow, cores blow out. No color anywhere yet. Output is HDR — values may exceed 1.0.

2 · Gradient

Three-stop position map: orange → pink → violet. Routed through magenta deliberately to avoid the muddy mid-zone you get from naive RGB lerp.

3 · Combine

Layered: soft base color × 0.22·L, colored mid bloom on L > 0.45, hot blowout on L > 1.3 toward white. Each cell has its own brightness and hue offset around the broad gradient.

4 · Tone map

Exponential exposure compresses HDR to display range. Saturation lift after tone-map keeps highlights from going pastel. Gamma 1/2.2 last.
